## 🔒 Privacy Policy

### Privacy Policy for Docs Template Gallery New Tab

This Chrome extension does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

### What the extension does

- Replaces the browser’s New Tab page with a gallery of Google Docs templates

- Displays static thumbnail images

- Provides links that allow users to make copies of Google Docs into their own Google accounts

### Data handling

- No personal information is collected

- No browsing history is tracked

- No analytics, cookies, or third-party trackers are used

- No data is sent to external servers

### Local storage

The extension may use Chrome’s local storage only to save user preferences (such as favorites) on the user’s own device. This data never leaves the browser and is not shared.

### Third-party services

Links in the extension may open Google Docs. Any interaction with Google services is governed by Google’s own privacy policies.

### Changes

This privacy policy may be updated if the extension’s functionality changes.

If you have questions, you may contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.
